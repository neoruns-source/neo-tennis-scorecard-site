Neo Tennis Scorecard

Tennis scoring and spoken score calls for Apple Watch

English · 中文 · Privacy Policy

What it is

A tennis scorekeeper that runs only on Apple Watch. Swipe up to score a point for yourself, swipe down to score for your opponent. It handles 15/30/40, deuce, tie-break and set wins, and offers four match formats: one set, best of 3, best of 5, and fast 4.

Requirements

  • Apple Watch running watchOS 26.5 or later
  • No iPhone app required, no account, no internet connection

FAQ

  • I scored the wrong point. Tap the undo button on the score screen; you can step back point by point.
  • I don't hear the spoken scores. First check that the speech toggle at the bottom of the menu is on, then check the volume in Settings → Sounds & Haptics on your watch. On a noisy court the watch speaker may not be loud enough — Bluetooth headphones work better.
  • How do I know who just scored? Every point gives haptic feedback: your point is one short, heavy tap; your opponent's point is a short run of light taps. You can also glance at the score — the half that just scored glows for a few seconds.
  • What happens if I pick another format? Choosing a format discards the current match. Switch formats after the match.
  • Does the app need the internet? No. It never uploads anything. See the Privacy Policy.

Neo Tennis Scorecard · 中文

Apple Watch 网球记分与语音报分

这是什么

一款只在 Apple Watch 上运行的网球记分 App：上滑记自己得分、下滑记对手得分，自动处理 15/30/40、平分（Deuce）、抢七与盘胜，支持单盘、3 盘 2 胜、5 盘 3 胜、快速 4 局四种赛制，并可语音报分。

系统要求

  • Apple Watch，watchOS 26.5 或更高版本
  • 无需 iPhone 端 App，无需注册账号，无需联网

常见问题

  • 分数记错了怎么办？计分页点撤销按钮，可逐分回退到任意历史比分。
  • 听不到语音报分？先确认菜单页底部的语音开关已打开，再检查手表「设置 → 声音与触感」里的音量；在户外嘈杂环境中，手表扬声器可能不足以听清，建议连接蓝牙耳机。
  • 怎么知道刚才谁得分？每记一分都会有一次触觉反馈：你得分是一记短促重响，对手得分是一段连续轻点；同时得分那一侧的半区会亮几秒，抬腕即可看到。
  • 换赛制会怎样？重新选择赛制会放弃当前这场比赛的分数，请在赛后更换。
  • App 需要联网吗？不需要，也不会上传任何数据。详见隐私政策。
